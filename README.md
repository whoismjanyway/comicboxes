# Comic Boxes

### 👉 [Open Comic Boxes](https://whoismjanyway.github.io/comicboxes/)

**https://whoismjanyway.github.io/comicboxes/**

A simple comic reader for **iPhone, iPad and Android**. Keep your comics in boxes, like a comic
shop's long boxes, with boxes inside boxes, and read them with pinch-to-zoom. Works offline.

- **Add whole folders:** subfolders become boxes inside boxes.
- **Formats:** CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT, PDF, EPUB, ZIP and folders of pictures. Even 1 GB files work on a phone.
- **Reader:** pinch and double-tap zoom, swipe or tap to turn pages, two-page view on tablets, Manga mode.
- **Black or white theme**, a Keep reading row, and a Settings page to arrange everything.
- **Send boxes or issues to another device** on the same Wi-Fi by scanning a QR code. Never uses mobile data.
- Your comics stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

## Put it on your phone

1. Open the link above on your phone.
2. **iPhone / iPad:** in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
   **Android:** in Chrome tap **⋮ → Add to home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Open it once from the new icon while on Wi-Fi so it saves itself for offline use.
   **Settings → Offline use** shows a green dot when it's ready.

Add your comics from **inside** the home-screen app: on iPhone, the app and Safari keep separate libraries.
